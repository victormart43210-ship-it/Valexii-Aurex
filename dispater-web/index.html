ONE STRAIGHTFORWARD PROCESS

From scattered records to review-ready evidence.

01

Map your records

Identify the tickets, invoices, rate sheets and documents your team already maintains. Determine which sources are relevant to the requested review.

02

Find the gaps

Compare linked records and surface candidate missing invoices, inconsistent rates, unreconciled payments and evidence-index gaps.

03

Review with a human

Managers resolve exceptions; qualified tax and transportation professionals determine what requirements apply. AI explanations never certify compliance.

04

Prepare the packet

Organize a traceable document index and an unresolved-items list for human review. No automatic filing, submission, dispatch or payment.
