## From Excel clutter to evidence you can follow

Excel is powerful. But a workbook can become difficult to trust when different people maintain different versions, invoice IDs are inconsistent, formulas silently drift, or source documents are stored somewhere else.

A DISPATER managed pilot can be scoped to help teams **prepare, standardize and optimize spreadsheet-based workflows**, with permission and human review. Proposed work includes:

- Map existing XLSX/CSV exports and agreed field definitions; identify missing columns, duplicates, inconsistent dates, and mismatched IDs.
- Establish consistent workbook templates, validation rules, controlled formulas and traceable version history.
- Compare dispatch tickets, mileage, labor, equipment, rate sheets, invoices and payments to flag candidate exceptions.
- Keep links back to the relevant source record, document version, applicable rate and reviewer decision.
- Produce reviewable exception queues and export-ready summaries for an accountant or auditor to assess.

**A tidy spreadsheet is not proof by itself.** Source records, permissions, appropriate retention and review still matter. Automated matching can highlight discrepancies; it cannot independently certify financial or regulatory compliance.

## Prepared when questions come

Audit readiness is a continuous habit: keep records organized, note what is missing, preserve the source trail, and make approval responsibility clear.

That can make it easier to respond to customer disputes, internal reviews, accountant requests, insurer inquiries, and **IRS or DOT record requests relevant to your business**. The actual requirements vary by operation and jurisdiction. DISPATER does **not** replace counsel, certified auditors, accountants or compliance specialists and does not guarantee an audit outcome.

## What the planned workflow looks like

**1. Connect permitted records.** Identify which existing dispatch, spreadsheet, accounting and document sources the business authorizes for a scoped pilot.

**2. Organize and qualify.** Standardize record identifiers, preserve provenance, and keep missing or stale evidence clearly marked.

**3. Review exceptions.** Surface candidate missing tickets, duplicate charges, rate mismatches and incomplete supporting documents.

**4. Explain with XAELI.** NOW / CHANGED / NEXT / WHY / PROOF connect each explanation to its underlying sources, uncertainties and review status.

**5. Approve with people.** Managers or accounting staff make the actual decisions. AI does not authorize payments, certify audits, or issue final financial conclusions on its own.

**6. Prepare a clear packet.** Gather source-linked records and reviewer notes for the appropriate human professional to examine.
