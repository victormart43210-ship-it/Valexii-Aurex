---

## I. THE 8 EXECUTIVE COMMAND MODULES

1. **Executive Overview**: High-level KPIs (Underbilling, Audit Readiness Score, Unresolved Exceptions, Confirmed Recovery) and interactive findings explorer.
2. **Revenue Recovery**: Tabular discrepancy ledger tracking scenarios, exposure amounts, and review statuses.
3. **Audit Readiness**: DOT/FMCSA regulatory inventory status and expiration tracking.
4. **Contract Intelligence**: Active Master Service Agreement pricing schedules and amendments.
5. **Operations**: Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) to field telemetry and geofence matching.
6. **Evidence Explorer**: SHA-256 digests and document cryptographic provenance links.
7. **Corrective Actions**: Operational task assignments required to close billing gaps.
8. **Financial Outcomes**: Segregated financial breakdown maintaining human authority gates.

---

## II. XAELI 5-STEP EXPLANATION INTEGRATION
Every finding displays the canonical reasoning sequence:
- **NOW**: Current operational or ledger observation.
- **CHANGED**: Delta detected against benchmark or baseline record.
- **NEXT**: Recommended human corrective action.
- **WHY**: Financial risk, contractual breach, or compliance penalty prevented.
- **PROOF**: Cryptographic SHA-256 digest or source log citation.
