## Executive Summary

DISPATER.AI is an evidence-driven industrial revenue assurance and operational intelligence platform. Industrial service providers lose 3% to 5% of gross billable revenue annually due to disconnected field tickets, missing purchase orders, rate sheet discrepancies, duplicate supplier invoicing, and unbilled line items.

DISPATER reconciles operational field records against commercial rate sheets and purchase orders deterministically, generating immutable SHA-256 evidence digests and human-in-the-loop review queues.

---

## Core Value Proposition

1. **Immediate Revenue Leakage Identification:** Automatically detects unbilled field work, rate mismatches, and missing purchase orders before billing cycles close.
2. **Deterministic Evidence Lineage:** Every discrepancy is backed by canonical SHA-256 hash digests (`Evidence -> Qualification -> Reality -> Authority -> Effect`).
3. **Zero Automated Financial Posting:** Models and algorithms generate hypotheses and exception queues (`AUTHORITY = NONE`), leaving financial authorization strictly with human managers.
4. **Rapid Onboarding:** Ingests standard CSV ticket feeds, ERP exports, and rate sheets with zero mandatory API redesign.
