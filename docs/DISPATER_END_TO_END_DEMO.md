# DISPATER.AI End-to-End Synthetic Towing Demonstration Guide

## Executive Overview
This guide provides the complete step-by-step operational script for demonstrating DISPATER's audit readiness and office management workflow to prospective towing company clients.

## Synthetic Demonstration Sequence

### Step 1: Open DISPATER Console (`/dispater`)
- View the unified commercial operations suite featuring:
  1. Commercial Towing Field Record Audit Console
  2. Operational Office Action Queue
  3. Digital Audit Binder Export Tool

### Step 2: Audit Execution & XAELI Findings
- Click **Initiate Authenticated Audit** in the Towing Audit Console.
- Observe backend processing status and review 5-step XAELI findings:
  - **NOW**: Record inventory status
  - **CHANGED**: Snapshot delta
  - **NEXT**: Required human review actions
  - **WHY**: Bounded reasoning scope
  - **PROOF**: SHA-256 evidence ledger verification

### Step 3: Office Work Queue Dispositions
- In the Office Work Queue, assign staff members and apply dispositions:
  - `ACKNOWLEDGED`: Issue logged by office staff
  - `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`: Missing document requested from driver
  - `RESOLVED_BY_HUMAN_REVIEW`: Exception reviewed and approved by human reviewer

### Step 4: Digital Audit Binder Export
- Click **Export Audit Binder (.json)** to generate the complete synthetic audit package with SHA-256 content manifests and verifier signatures.
